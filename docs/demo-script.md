# Demo video script (target 3:30)

Limit: 3–4 minutes; over 4 minutes is penalised. Record on the deployed link, not localhost. Screen plus voice-over; show the patient on camera only during the session.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing page | "After an amputation, recovery happens at home, alone. Most patients stop their exercises, and the doctor only finds out at the next visit. Langkah closes that gap." |
| 0:20 | Scenario **Pak Budi: pain is rising** → step 1 (switch to ID to show Indonesian) | "Pak Budi, 58, lost his left leg below the knee to diabetes. His physio set today's plan. The app speaks his language, in simple words." |
| 0:40 | **Start exercises**, camera + mic on | "The voice coach is Gemini Live. It knows his plan, his left leg, and his goal from earlier sessions." Let the coach greet, ask pain, ask phantom limb pain. |
| 1:10 | Bridging set: skeleton on screen, counter going up, coach says a form cue | "The camera counts reps on the device with MediaPipe. The coach stays quiet during the set and only speaks the cues." |
| 1:50 | Second exercise (cut the rest if needed), pain after: say "8" | "Pain went up to 8." |
| 2:05 | Summary: "Rest today and contact your clinic", "Your care team has been told" | "The next step comes from fixed rules, not the AI: predictable and safe." |
| 2:20 | Turn on **Physio dashboard**: high alert at the top | "On the physio's side, the alert is already there." |
| 2:35 | **Generate** AI brief | "Before the visit, Gemini writes a brief from his sessions, pain trend, phantom pain and what he told the coach. The numbers come from the app; the physio can edit the text." |
| 2:55 | Edit plan: lower reps, note → Save as new version | "The physio lowers the dose." |
| 3:05 | Step 4 **Next day**: new plan and note on his phone | "The next day, Pak Budi sees the new plan. The loop is closed." |
| 3:15 | Optional 5 s: skin check result, or Pak Liem in Chinese | "Skin checks and three languages are built in too." |
| 3:20 | Landing / title card | "Langkah: Gemini Live, Firebase, Firestore and Cloud Run, for patients who need to keep stepping forward." |

Tips: record the session part separately and cut it; do a dry run first so the coach's memories are in place; use **Reset demo** before the final take.
